# Ready Together

Ready Together is a free, US-focused household emergency preparedness planner. Choose who you’re preparing for, what risks you face, and how long you want your supplies to last. The planner turns those choices into a buying guide, supply targets, and a printable plan you can keep for an outage or evacuation.

**[Open the interactive planner](https://readytogether.info/)** · [Buying guide](https://readytogether.info/#buying-guide) · [Final plan & PDF](https://readytogether.info/#final-plan)

## Build your first plan

Select **Take the tour** on the planner’s home screen to walk through the live controls and finish at the PDF download. You can change settings during the tour or use **Skip tour** to continue on your own.

1. In **Your starting point**, choose **Immediate prep** if you want to act now or **Long-term prep** to build in stages. Immediate prep lets you select seasons. Long-term prep keeps your selected hazards and adds maintenance tasks.
2. Choose your **Prepper experience**. If this is your first plan, select **Beginner** and start with the **Core** supply goal.
3. Pick **1 adult**, **2 adults**, or **Family · 2 adults + 2 children**, then edit the counts for your actual household. Count infants separately from children eating solid food. Add pets, phones, home type and any care, mobility, dietary, well-water or transport needs.
4. Pick a region, then check or uncheck hazards for where you live. Choose what you need backup communications to do. If a device or pet enclosure needs electricity, use **Portable power and solar generators** to enter the specific loads.
5. Under **Your supply targets**, enter the water and food you already have. Open the **Buying guide** for examples, quantities and setup checks. Mark actions **To do**, **Done**, or **Not needed** as you work.
6. Choose what to include under **What do you need from this report?** Then open **Final plan & PDF**, review the preview, and download. You can download while tasks are still open.

### Experience and supply duration

**Experience** sets the depth of guidance. The **supply goal** sets the number of days. For example, a beginner can work toward 14 days using complete days of meals, and an experienced household can use detailed power and communication guidance for a 3-day plan.

| Prepper experience | What the buying guide adds |
| --- | --- |
| **Beginner** | Plain-language package checks, normal meal portions, and first steps. The three-day no-cook basket shows how to choose canned food, fruit, cereal, shelf-stable milk and snacks. Get shopping quantities for water, hygiene, first aid, medicines, lights, radios and evacuation supplies. |
| **Intermediate** | Pantry rotation and backups, a first-aid refill inventory, measured power use, a basic roadside kit, and equipment options for the selected communication goal. |
| **Advanced** | A pantry trial, critical-load and recharging tests, device and care handoffs, detailed communications shopping lists, and vehicle kits matched to ordinary roads, remote roads or trails. |

| Supply goal | Duration | Work to plan for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core** | 3 days | Safe water, ready-to-eat meals, care supplies, lights, communications and an evacuation plan. |
| **Advanced** | 14 days | Add rotation, backup power, temporary sanitation and practice drills. |
| **Super prep** | 30 days | Add resupply, maintenance, replacement supplies and a trusted support network. |

**Example.** A beginner planning for two adults and two children selects **Core** and reports 5 gallons of safe water on hand. With no pets, no extra water needs, and **Extreme heat** unselected, the calculator shows 12 gallons total and 7 to add. The food worksheet gives 12 portions for each meal category across the three days, such as 12 lunch protein portions. The planner asks for complete days of meals already stored. [CDC emergency water guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/about/how-to-create-and-store-an-emergency-water-supply.html)

### Tailor the equipment and care lists

- **Communications.** Everyone starts with emergency broadcasts and a check-in plan. Choose nearby voice, across-town contact or travel outside cell coverage to see relevant options. The deeper guidance covers FRS, GMRS or compatible mesh equipment where applicable, and satellite options for remote travel. Bring the guide to the store for quantities, compatibility checks and the test to run at your actual locations.
- **Power.** Start with lights and phone charging. For other loads, select **Include a portable power setup in my plan**. Add measured daily energy or enter watts and equivalent full-power hours. Presets include a reptile enclosure, refrigerator/freezer, router/modem and USB charging, with room to add your own devices. Compare portable station capacity, no-sun runtime, and estimated solar recharge. Check outlet watts, startup surge, connectors and actual runtime before relying on a setup.
- **Travel.** Intermediate plans can include a roadside kit. In Advanced, select **Where will you drive?** to add equipment for ordinary roads, remote roads or off-pavement travel. The kit only brings in recovery gear for the trail option. If you need help with evacuation transport, the planner prompts for backup rides.
- **Household care and local risks.** Review the worksheets for regular medicines, first aid, infant feeding, pets, accessibility and selected hazards. The plan can help you organize questions for a pharmacist or care team and record evacuation and backup arrangements.

## Trusted Contact Authentication

Under **Your communication plan**, open **Trusted Contact Authentication → Open authentication setup**. It is available for every household and experience level. The warm amber guidance card, five-step quick start, and expandable security advice follow the planner’s existing visual design. The eight-step tour now includes this panel. The main preparedness checklist includes a **Set up a trusted-contact code and callback plan** action with the usual **To do / Done / Not needed** states.

### Set up and use a shared code

1. Install a compatible authenticator on each trusted phone. **2FAS Auth** supports standard TOTP enrollment. **VIP Access** offers a QR/App Code credential wallet; exact custom-QR support and controls depend on the app/version. If rejected, use 2FAS. The built-in VIP Credential ID is unrelated to this shared entry.
2. Prefer an in-person setup with a trusted computer showing the QR and both phones nearby. Enter a contact/group nickname, select **Generate new shared key & QR**, and scan the **same** QR with both phones. Generate once per shared entry, not once per phone. Prefer a separate key per contact; all members of a group can generate its group code.
3. Compare the six-digit codes in both authenticators and Ready Together. They must match and rotate together every 30 seconds. Wait for the next code if close to the boundary. Verify automatic date/time first; then test airplane mode. No server clock is contacted.
4. On a single phone, reveal the private setup key for manual authenticator entry: **TOTP / SHA-1 / 6 digits / 30 seconds**. Avoid screenshots/cloud photo backups. If you copy a key yourself, treat the clipboard and any device clipboard sync as sensitive. For an already shared key, expand **Use an existing private setup key**. Only dedicated trusted-contact keys belong here; never reuse a bank/work login seed.
5. During a suspicious call, ask the caller for their current code and **silently compare** it with yours. Do not volunteer your own code first. Even after a match, independently call a **previously trusted number** before sending money, disclosing sensitive information or acting on an emergency claim. Never use a new callback number supplied by the caller.

The session can hold up to 12 labeled entries. Select an entry to view its current code, countdown and enrollment QR. **Hide private codes**, **Remove this session entry**, and **Clear all session keys** control what remains in this browser session. Private displays hide when the tab is backgrounded and on printing; **Show private codes** reveals the active session again. All entries are cleared on page exit/reload and confirmed planner reset. Authenticator copies remain usable after the browser session is cleared.

### Security and privacy boundaries

- Keys use 20 cryptographically random bytes (160 bits), generated with `crypto.getRandomValues`. Codes use Web Crypto HMAC-SHA-1, six digits, 30-second steps, Unix time, and the standard `otpauth://totp/` enrollment URI. Manual imports validate canonical, unpadded Base32 secrets containing 16–64 bytes. SHA-1 is the standard TOTP compatibility setting here, not a password hash.
- Keys, contact nicknames, QR payloads and rotating codes stay outside planner state. This module makes **no network requests** and writes **no localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, cookies or secret exports**. Persistence is intentionally unavailable; keep ongoing entries in your chosen authenticator. No QR image service, analytics, camera permission or CDN is used by the authentication module. The QR encoder is bundled locally with its MIT license in `dist/vendor/`.
- Planner backup JSON, buying guides, personalized PDFs, and ordinary page printing contain **public instructions only**, never private session data. Existing medical inventory opt-ins are unchanged. Any text a user deliberately types in general household notes remains subject to the existing notes/export behavior: do not paste secrets there.
- Matching codes verify **possession of a shared secret, not identity**. This is a supplemental family check, not a phishing-resistant protocol, trusted identity service, login system, emergency dispatcher or replacement for independent verification. Codes may be guessed, intercepted, briefly replayed, or relayed live. There is no centralized replay prevention, rate limiting or revocation. Group keys do not identify individual group members.
- Browser memory is not a secure enclave and JavaScript cannot guarantee memory erasure. A compromised device, same-origin script, malicious extension, unlocked phone, camera or screen capture can expose keys/codes. A restrictive same-origin Content Security Policy and no-referrer policy reduce exposure; they cannot make a compromised site trustworthy. Review the authenticator’s own app-lock and backup/sync settings separately.
- If a phone/key is lost or exposed, stop trusting that entry. Verify the other participants independently, create a fresh key, re-enroll everyone, and delete the old entry from every authenticator. Clearing the web session does **not** revoke existing copies. There is no recovery account or server-side revocation.
- On open radio channels, others can hear and relay codes. This feature adds no encryption, radio path, coverage or licensing exemption. Use a prearranged fallback when callback is unavailable and defer sensitive decisions. Do not delay lifesaving care to obtain a code; use local emergency services for immediate danger.

### Offline PWA support

Version 1.10 adds a service worker to the existing PWA manifest/icons. Open Ready Together online and wait for **App files ready for offline use on this device** under Trusted Contact Authentication. Then test airplane mode, including reopening the planner and downloading its PDF. Local HTTPS or localhost is required for Web Crypto/service workers. A generic HTTP LAN address and a downloaded `file://` page are not supported offline installations.

The worker caches a fixed allowlist of public app assets, the PDF generator, source directory, icons and both static reference PDFs. It never receives authentication secrets, handles secret messages, or performs runtime caching of arbitrary requests or query strings. A successful install writes a public completion marker only after all files are cached. Existing tabs keep their current app version; close all Ready Together tabs and reopen online after an update. Versioned caches are cleaned on activation. The cache is not guaranteed permanent: browser eviction, private browsing, insufficient space or OS policies may remove it. Recheck and retest before an outage. An installed PWA icon alone does not prove offline readiness.

Offline enrollment and browser codes work during the current session, with an accurate clock. **Browser keys still disappear on reload**, online or offline. Enroll an authenticator before leaving; use its stored entry for ongoing verification. This feature does not transmit or synchronize secrets between devices. The two people must securely exchange the same key themselves.

## Emergency medical preparedness

Open **Medical** in the navigation or scroll to **Emergency medical preparedness**. The panel uses the current household, experience, medical-power flag, checklist statuses and PDF selection.

| Existing experience setting | Medical depth |
| --- | --- |
| Beginner | Essential adult OTC choices, first aid, pediatric plans when applicable, regular prescriptions and basic training |
| Intermediate | Advanced medical planning, optional clinician-reviewed standby prescription kits, bleeding-control training and appropriate monitoring tools |
| Advanced | Expert continuity planning, redundant critical equipment and supplies matched to the skills you report |

Supply duration remains Core (3 days), Advanced (14 days), or Super prep (30 days). Prescription reserves are discussed with the prescriber and pharmacist, aiming for 14–30 days where feasible. They are recorded per person and prescription. Medicines are never multiplied into a treatment dose or assumed safe for a child.

The buying guides distinguish adult OTC products, regular prescription continuity, prescribed rescue medicines, pediatric supplies and optional upgrades. First-aid consumables use a starter kit per four household members, rounded up, with replenishment based on use. OTC packages and cost ranges are shopping allowances, not dosing schedules. Individual products may be unsuitable for a person’s health conditions or other medicines.

Under **Tailor medical supplies and training**, add optional dental or veterinary supplies. Mark completed bleeding-control training to see matching trauma supplies in Intermediate or Advanced. Chest seals appear only at Expert medical depth when you also report specific chest-seal training. The planner records your declaration and optional training date. It does not certify skills.

JaseCase, Prepare Medical’s US offering, and Contingency Medical Ready Pack Plus are examples of services arranging prescriptions through health review and pharmacy dispensing. The module compares their published contents, approximate cost, support and limits. Listed provider/manufacturer prices were checked November 18, 2026. Other prices are labeled planning allowances. Confirm current state availability, patient eligibility, prescribing-clinician and dispensing-pharmacy licensing, final contents, follow-up terms and price before purchase. Provider pages describe their processes; this project has not independently audited their clinicians or every pharmacy partner.

Only a licensed clinician can authorize and guide prescription use. Never self-prescribe antibiotics, share a prescription kit or use leftover antibiotics to treat a new illness. Keep the prescriber’s written directions and contact route. Emergency symptoms require emergency care. The guide includes care escalation, offline references, FDA and CDC safeguards, Red Cross first aid and ACS Stop the Bleed resources.

### Medication inventory and reminders

Expand **Medication inventory and refill reminders**. You can enter up to 40 medicines or supplies with an optional household alias, type, name, strength/formulation, quantity with units, expiration, refill/review date and storage requirements. Keep prescribed directions and care contacts with private care records. Names, birth dates, diagnoses and identification numbers are not requested.

Two independent controls manage the inventory.

- **Keep my filled inventory in this browser** saves entries in unencrypted browser storage. It starts off. Without it, entries last for the current page session and disappear on reload.
- **Include my filled inventory in PDFs and editable backups** adds entries to personalized PDF and JSON exports. It starts off. The separate filled-inventory download becomes available when this option is on.

A blank inventory PDF is always available from the panel. Download it for handwriting, or opt into a filled PDF for an offline copy. A filled inventory can be included with any report selection, including Action plan only. The general **Print this page** view hides the private inventory panel; use the inventory download to print that list.

Refill/review and expiration alerts appear for overdue dates, dates due today and dates within 30 days when the planner is open. Choose a next household stock-review date and review monthly. No background notifications, email, calendar integration or quantity-based run-out calculation is provided. An absence of date alerts does not validate stock or storage. Offline use requires a completed public-app cache and an airplane-mode test, or downloading and testing the PDF/reference files before an outage.

**Done** and **Not needed** medical selections use the same state as the action checklist. Remaining-shopping summaries omit those completed/skipped medical items. Detailed medical PDF worksheets retain their status labels for review. Pediatric products appear only for households with infants or children and always require an individual age/weight/formulation check. Adult medicines and adult prescription kits are never assigned to children automatically.

Medical actions are grouped in expandable sections of the main checklist, and the tour includes the medical panel before the PDF step. The personalized report includes medical worksheets and clickable product/source links when shopping content is selected. The two prewritten reference PDFs remain separate reading resources. The live medical module and personalized export carry the updated material.

## How the planner calculates targets

| Estimate | Calculation or rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Stored water** | `((adults + children + infants) × gallons per person per day + pets × gallons per pet per day) × days`. Beginner starts at 1 US gallon per person per day and uses a visible 0.5-gallon planning buffer when extreme heat or extra water needs are selected. Intermediate and Advanced use your entered rate. Replace the 0.25 gal/pet/day starter value with your animals’ actual needs. |
| **Food** | Beginner counts complete household meal-days. Intermediate and Advanced use `(adults × adult kcal/day + children × child kcal/day) × days`. The editable 2,000 and 1,600 kcal defaults are inventory assumptions. Infant feeding is tracked separately. Switching experience preserves both inventories as separate inputs. |
| **Supply gap** | `max(0, target − on hand)`. The shopping guide rounds the remaining water up when purchasing whole one-gallon jugs. |
| **Quick battery estimate** | Nominal capacity in Wh = `daily Wh × days ÷ usable fraction`. Runtime in days = `station Wh × usable fraction ÷ daily Wh`. The estimate assumes zero recharge. |
| **Portable power** | The device’s measured Wh/day takes priority over `rated W × equivalent full-power hours`. Nominal capacity in Wh = `total Wh/day × backup hours ÷ 24 ÷ usable fraction`, rounded up. Complete daily-energy inputs are required for the estimate. |
| **Solar recharge** | Daily Wh = `lower of panel watts and station input limit × equivalent peak sun hours × yield fraction`. With the station input left blank, the estimate uses the entered panel watts. Verify the actual station limit and panel compatibility. |
| **Checklist progress** | `Done ÷ applicable tasks`. Tasks marked **Not needed** are excluded from the denominator. This measures progress through the checklist. |

Water quantities use US gallons. Regional presets are broad prompts, and town/state is a label. Check official local alerts, maps, routes and evacuation instructions for your location. Find links for medical, water, food, radio and power guidance in the [source directory](https://readytogether.info/sources.html).

## Public-site usage measurement

Container **GTM-PBJZX3NJ** is loaded by `dist/analytics.html` inside a hidden iframe sandboxed with **allow-scripts only**. The omitted `allow-same-origin` permission gives it an opaque origin: it cannot read the parent DOM, planner storage, medical inventory, contact labels, QR codes or shared keys. The only frame-to-parent message is a fixed loaded/unavailable diagnostic; no message or data is sent from the planner and no private form/click events are sent. The parent CSP continues to disallow Google scripts and connections in the planner itself. The frame has its own hash-authorized loader and narrowly scoped Google endpoints. Do not remove the sandbox or add `allow-same-origin`.

Google Consent Mode defaults analytics/ad storage and advertising permissions to **denied**, with fixed public page location/title, blank referrer and ad personalization disabled. Compatible Google Analytics tags can send limited cookieless visit signals; they do not get normal cookie-based user/session reporting. Google still receives network metadata such as IP address and browser information; this is not anonymous or zero-network operation. The analytics frame is deliberately excluded from the offline cache. Offline operation, ad blockers or blocked Google requests must not affect the planner.

The supplied **GTM-** ID identifies a Tag Manager container, not a **G-** Analytics measurement ID. Configure and publish a Google tag/GA4 destination in that container to enable reports. Use the page-view trigger; do not add DOM/form capture, session replay, user IDs, medical events or contact events. The loader runs only inside an opaque-origin iframe and does nothing when its URL is opened directly. Tags can see only the empty analytics frame, so parent-page click/form triggers and parent Tag Assistant detection will not behave like a top-level snippet. In GA4/Tag Manager, keep `page_location=https://readytogether.info/`, `page_title=Ready Together`, `page_referrer=` and disable enhanced form/outbound/search/history measurement; no private inputs should ever be forwarded. Container-side overrides require review. This implementation verifies installation/loading, not access to your Google account or receipt of events in your Analytics property.

To remove measurement, remove the `ready-analytics` iframe and `analytics.html`, update the public-file cache version, and redeploy. The earlier dedicated Trusted Contact rollback remains pinned to its documented pre-authentication app and also removes this later analytics addition if used.

## Privacy and reuse

The planner keeps information in browser storage and generates your PDF on your device. It has no account or app database. Public visits use Google Tag Manager in a separate sandboxed frame; the tracker cannot read private planner fields or authentication data. The host can receive ordinary request metadata. Older version-1 plans without an experience selection reopen in Advanced mode to preserve their technical inputs. New plans start in Beginner mode. The editable backup is versioned JSON with `version`, `exportedAt` and `plan` fields.

Follow local emergency instructions, product manuals and individualized care guidance. Treat household exports as private and share the planner or reference guides with others.
